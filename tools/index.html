Input Guide and Data Sources

For consistency, use diluted EPS on an LTM / TTM basis. For cyclical, commodity, or temporarily distorted businesses, use normalized EPS instead of a one-year peak or trough. Expected growth should usually be a 3–5 year annual EPS growth estimate.

Input Preferred Data Where to Look
EPS Diluted EPS, LTM / TTM. Use normalized EPS when the last year is distorted. Company filings, annual report, Yahoo Finance statistics, StockAnalysis financials.
Growth Expected annual EPS growth over 3–5 years. Analyst consensus pages, company guidance, historical EPS trend, StockCrafted internal assumptions.
P/E Current trailing P/E. If set to 0, the calculator uses Price ÷ EPS. Broker platform, Yahoo Finance statistics, StockAnalysis ratios.
Dividend Yield Forward yield is preferred for stable dividend payers; trailing yield is acceptable if used consistently. Company dividend page, broker platform, dividend history source.
AAA Yield Moody's Seasoned Aaa Corporate Bond Yield or equivalent AAA corporate yield. FRED or another fixed-income data source.
Open Yahoo Finance Statistics Open StockAnalysis Statistics Open FRED AAA Yield Open SEC Filings Search

Interpretation

This calculator combines Graham's earnings-growth valuation with Lynch's PEG logic. Treat it as a quick valuation anchor, not a final target price. High-quality compounders, cyclicals, banks, REITs, and temporarily depressed earnings often require a fuller model.

Annualised Figures

Annualised figures are mechanical comparisons. They assume that similar cycle returns could be repeated over a full year. That assumption is useful for comparison, but it is not a forecast, guarantee, or invitation to annoy probability itself.

Wheel Logic

The Wheel is modelled as one sequence: sell a cash-secured put, accept assignment if it occurs, sell a covered call, then either remain long or exit at the call strike. Premiums and dividends are included only in the paths where they are actually earned.

Compounding Projection Note

Compounding projections are simplified mechanical estimates. They assume that similar average income yields can be repeated and reinvested over time. Actual results will vary because option premiums, volatility, assignment, dividends, prices, liquidity, and available opportunities change.

Annualised Figures

Annualised figures assume that comparable cycle returns could be repeated continuously over a full year. They are useful for comparing setups, but they are not forecasts, guarantees, or realistic promises of future return.

These calculators are educational tools. They do not provide financial advice, recommendations, tax guidance, or guarantees of future results. Options involve risk and require disciplined position sizing, liquidity awareness, and full understanding of assignment obligations.
